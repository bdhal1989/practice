“A load balancer sits in front of application servers and distributes incoming requests to multiple servers using strategies like round robin, 
least connections, or IP hashing. There are mainly two types of load balancers: L4 and L7. 
L4 operates at the transport layer of the OSI model and routes traffic based on IP and port, 
while L7 operates at the application layer and can make routing decisions based on HTTP data 
like URLs or headers.”
